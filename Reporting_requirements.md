# Student Reporting Requirements (Grant-Funded Research)

This document defines the **mandatory reporting requirements** for all students, trainees, and research personnel supported by, contributing to, or affiliated with **grant-funded research activities** conducted under the ZeD Lab (Zero Knowledge Discovery Laboratory).

These reports serve as formal records for:
- Grant compliance and progress tracking
- Mentoring and performance evaluation
- Continuity of research programs
- Institutional, sponsor, and audit requirements

All reports must be **accurate, complete, and submitted on time**.

---

## 1. Reporting Cadence

### 1.1 Annual Report (Required)

All students must submit an **annual progress report** covering the preceding 12-month period.

This report is mandatory for:
- Grant-funded trainees
- Students receiving salary, stipend, or tuition support
- Students listed on funded aims, milestones, or effort statements

### 1.2 Interim and Ad-Hoc Reports (As Required)

Additional reports may be required:
- At **6-month intervals**
- Prior to grant renewals, continuations, or milestones
- Upon request by the PI, project lead, or sponsor
- During onboarding, remediation, or role transitions

Failure to submit required reports may affect funding continuation, authorship eligibility, or lab affiliation.

---

## 2. Required Report Sections

Each report must include **all sections below**. Omission of required sections renders the report incomplete.

---

### 2.1 Project Description and Current Status

Provide a concise description of **all active projects** to which you have contributed during the reporting period.

For each project, include:
- Project title
- Funding source(s), if applicable
- Scientific or technical objective
- Your role and level of effort
- Current status (e.g., exploratory, data collection, modeling, validation, manuscript preparation)

Projects must be clearly distinguishable when multiple efforts are involved.

---

### 2.2 Goals and Planned Work

State concrete research objectives for the upcoming period:
- **Next 6 months** (interim reports)
- **Next 12 months** (annual reports)

Goals should be **specific, measurable, and aligned with funded aims**, such as:
- Methodological or theoretical developments
- Data acquisition or integration milestones
- Model development, validation, or benchmarking
- Manuscript submission or revision targets
- Software releases or internal deliverables

Purely aspirational or non-actionable statements should be avoided.

---

### 2.3 Publications and Manuscripts

List all scholarly outputs since the previous report.

#### A. Submitted or Published Works
Include:
- Full citation
- Journal or conference
- Submission and decision dates
- Current status (under review, accepted, published)
- Your authorship position

#### B. Manuscripts in Preparation
Include:
- Working title
- Target venue
- Current stage (outline, draft, revision)
- Expected submission timeline

---

### 2.4 Code, Software, and Computational Artifacts

Document all substantive software contributions, including:
- Public repositories (URLs required)
- Private or pre-release repositories
- Major pipelines, libraries, or analytical tools
- Significant contributions to shared codebases

For each item, briefly state:
- Purpose and functionality
- Current maturity level (prototype, production, archived)
- Relationship to funded aims, if applicable

---

### 2.5 Conceptual and Theoretical Contributions

Describe any **new conceptual, mathematical, or theoretical work** developed since the last report, including but not limited to:
- New problem formulations
- Modeling frameworks or abstractions
- Analytical results or derivations
- Proof sketches or formal arguments
- Novel connections between domains

This section is required even if no publication has yet resulted.

---

### 2.6 Presentations and Scholarly Dissemination

List all presentations delivered during the reporting period, including:
- Conference talks
- Poster presentations
- Workshops or invited seminars
- Internal lab or departmental talks

For each, include:
- Title
- Venue
- Date
- Format (oral, poster, invited)

---

### 2.7 Media Mentions and External Visibility

Report any external visibility related to your research, including:
- Media coverage or interviews
- Blog posts or technical articles
- Public datasets or benchmarks released
- Mentions in reports, policy documents, or external reviews
- Research-related public outreach or social media posts

Provide links where available.

---

## 3. Formatting and Submission

- Reports must be submitted as a **single Markdown or PDF document**
- All sections must be clearly labeled
- Supporting links, figures, or appendices may be included as needed
- Reports must be archived as instructed by the lab or project lead

Conciseness is encouraged, but completeness is mandatory.

---

## 4. Compliance and Use

These reports constitute official records used to:
- Demonstrate progress toward funded aims
- Support sponsor reporting and renewals
- Inform mentoring, evaluation, and authorship decisions
- Ensure continuity across personnel and project transitions

Reports are expected to be professional, factual, and reflective of actual work performed.
